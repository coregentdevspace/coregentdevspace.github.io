# Coregent Digital Studio

Apps for Atlassian Cloud, built on Atlassian Forge.

## Backlog Hygiene for Jira

Finds duplicate, obsolete and stale work items in your Jira backlog and suggests what to merge or close. Nothing changes until a Jira administrator approves.
